# LeadPilot AI — Founder-led paid pilot

## Offer
A manually operated sales follow-up setup for businesses with existing inbound enquiries. The five LeadPilot tools can prioritize supplied messages, identify overdue replies, draft responses, prepare meeting suggestions from verified availability, and create a sales briefing.

## Honest sales terms
This is a human-assisted pilot, not a self-service SaaS subscription. No automatic inbox sync, messaging, appointment booking, or CRM access is promised. The customer supplies authorized lead data and approves every outgoing message.

## First 3 customers
Offer a 7-day trial or a clearly scoped paid pilot. Quote fees individually, collect payments through a separately verified business payment method, and give a written scope and cancellation/refund terms. Do not claim the $9/$29/$79 SaaS plans can be purchased until Paystack subscriptions are active.

## Daily operating checklist
1. Ask the customer for a small sample of authorized, redacted conversations.
2. Rank leads and return a priority queue.
3. Draft follow-ups for approval; customer sends messages.
4. Prepare meetings only from actual supplied availability.
5. Report daily activity and customer-confirmed outcomes.
6. Record measurable response times, bookings and sales without inventing conversion numbers.

## Success criteria
At least three consenting pilot customers, a repeatable onboarding process, customer-approved case studies, and a validated price point before scaling paid acquisition.
